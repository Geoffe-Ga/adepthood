## What Adepthood is

Adepthood is a journal with optional structure around it: prompts, habits,
a practice timer, a reading course, and reflections generated by a language
model from what you have written.

## What Adepthood is not

**It is not care.** It is not therapy, counselling, medical advice, or a
medical device, and no part of it is a clinician. Reflections are produced
by a language model from your own writing; they can be wrong, and they are
not a professional opinion about you.

**It is not for an emergency.** If you are in danger or thinking about
harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line. The
app surfaces crisis and professional-care resources on every screen where
it might matter, and reaching a person is always the better move.

**Nothing it says should be acted on as instruction about medication.** Any
change to a prescription is between you and a prescriber.

## Your writing stays yours

You keep every right you have in what you write here. Nothing in these
terms transfers ownership of it, and nothing here permits using your
writing to train a model.

To run the service, Adepthood needs narrow permission to handle your
writing on your behalf: to store it, to show it back to you, to send a
non-Intimate entry to a language-model provider when you ask for a
reflection, and to replicate it to your own vault if you have configured
one. That permission exists only to provide the features you are using, it
extends no further, and it ends when you delete the content or the account.

The [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md) is the exact description of where
your writing goes. Where these terms and that page could be read

## Paying for things

Purchases are handled by **Gumroad**, which is the seller and takes the
payment. Adepthood verifies what you bought and credits it to your account:
a licence, or a pack of tokens that pays for model calls. Card details are
Gumroad's to handle and never reach Adepthood.

Refunds follow Gumroad's process. A refunded or revoked sale removes the
entitlement it granted.

Model calls consume tokens from your balance. A call that fails before the
model answers does not cost you the tokens; a call the model answers does,
whether or not you liked the answer.

You may instead supply your **own provider key** in Settings. That key
stays on your device, is used for the one call it is attached to, and is
never stored on the server — but the calls it makes are billed to your
account with that provider, and what they cost is between you and them.

does, your writing still saves — that is the floor the app is built on —
but a reflection may not arrive.
