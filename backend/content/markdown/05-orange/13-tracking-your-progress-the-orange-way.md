## Tracking Your Progress: The Orange Way

Because Orange loves data, track your breath retention times. After each round, write down how long you held your breath. Over weeks, the number will probably trend up—with off days, plateaus, and the odd unexplained dip. That's still your nervous system adapting; it just doesn't adapt in a straight line.

But also track subjective experience. How do you feel after the practice? More focused? Calmer? Energized? These qualitative notes are just as important as the numbers.

This is the marriage of quantitative and qualitative—the hallmark of good science.
