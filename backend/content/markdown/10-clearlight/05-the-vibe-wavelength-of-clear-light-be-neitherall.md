## The Mode of the Wavelength of Clear Light: Be (Both/Neither)

Clear Light introduces the sixth and final Mode: **Be (Both/Neither)**.

This is not Inhabit (building), Express (externalizing), Collaborate (co-creating), Integrate (synthesizing), or Absorb (receiving). This is simply *being*. Not as passivity. Not as withdrawal. But as the ground from which all the other modes arise.

Be (Both/Neither) is the recognition that you are not *doing* any of the modes—they are *happening through you*. You're making dinner. The chopping happens. The deciding-what-to-add happens. Look for the one doing it, and all you find is more chopping. You're not the actor. You're the stage. And the play that's unfolding is everything and nothing at once (Both)—and not reducible to either (Neither).

This is the mode of emptiness. It holds every other mode because none of them has a solid owner—and it isn't reducible to any of them. You can Inhabit when needed. Express when called. Collaborate when invited. Integrate when complexity arises. Absorb when the teaching comes. But you're not *stuck* in any of them. You're free to move fluidly because you're *none* of them—and all of them.

The Wavelength at Clear Light is the same six phases you've been working with since Beige. You're still *subject to* the Wavelength—nobody isn't. What changes is that you can see it while it's moving you. Some days that's surfing. Some days it's getting dumped by the wave and knowing it's a wave.

Rising, Peaking, Withdrawal, Diminishing, Bottoming Out, Restoration—they're all still here. You still cycle. You still have highs and lows. You'll still get swallowed by a phase. Less often, and for less long, because you've seen the whole pattern—and seeing it is what buys you a little room.

We'll explore the Wavelength in detail. But first, understand this: Clear Light doesn't transcend the cycle. It *illuminates* it. You see it so clearly that you stop being moved unconsciously by it. And that clarity is liberation.
