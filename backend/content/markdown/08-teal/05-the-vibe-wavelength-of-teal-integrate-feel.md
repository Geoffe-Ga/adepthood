## The Mode of the Wavelength of Teal: Integrate (Feel)

Teal continues the **Integrate** Mode but shifts from **Do** (Yellow) to **Feel**. Where Yellow integrated through analysis and discipline, Teal integrates through *receptivity*. You're not building synthesis—you're *allowing* it to emerge.

This is the Wavelength of the mystic. The artist in flow. The shaman in ceremony. You're working with forces larger than your conscious mind. You're trusting the intelligence of the body, the unconscious, the field.

Teal's Wavelength shares Yellow's Mode—both are Integrate—but the resemblance ends there. Where Yellow's Wavelength was focused and directed, Teal's is soft and receptive.

Teal has its own medicine and its own poison for each phase:

**Rising: Rx = Epiphany | OD = Delusion**

**Peaking: Rx = Gnosis | OD = Psychosis**

**Withdrawal: Rx = Receptivity | OD = Paranoia**

**Diminishing: Rx = Absorption | OD = Horror**

**Bottoming Out: Rx = Metabolism | OD = Despair**

**Restoration: Rx = Pattern-Seeking | OD = Belief Salience**

Yellow's medicine was things you *do*—Organize, Establish, Order. Teal's medicine is things that *happen to you*—Epiphany, Gnosis, Absorption. And Teal's poison is what happens when the personality grabs the wheel of an experience it was only supposed to receive. At Yellow, you were actively managing the Wavelength—noticing drift and correcting. At Teal, you're *flowing with* the Wavelength—trusting that the cycle will move on its own.

We'll explore the felt sense of each phase in the breakdown. But first, note this: Teal's practice is less about *controlling* attention and more about *surrendering* to awareness. You're not trying to make anything happen. You're just being present with what is.
