## The Witness and the Participator: Teal's Paradox

Here's the paradox Teal must hold: you're in touch with the True Self (the part of you that is eternal and chose this life) *and* you're fully living that life (engaging, feeling, acting, doing the dishes).

These seem contradictory. How can you be detached *and* engaged?

The answer is that you're not detached—you're *non-attached*. Detachment is withdrawal. Non-attachment is full engagement without clinging.

True Self connection doesn't make you cold or distant. It makes you *present*—because the eternal you wanted this life. You feel joy fully—but you don't grasp at it. You feel grief fully—but you don't drown in it. You engage completely—but you remember who chose the role.

This is the dancer and the dance. You're dancing (participating) *and* you remember who walked into the room to dance (the True Self). Both at once. Not alternating. Simultaneously.

This is hard to hold. It's what Teal trains.
