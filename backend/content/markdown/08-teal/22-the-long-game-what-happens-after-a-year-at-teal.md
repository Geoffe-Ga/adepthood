## The Long Game: What Happens After a Year at Teal

After a year of Dog Walkin' Shamanism (45 min, five days a week if you can manage it) and Baby Waterfall Conventions (daily), here's what often shifts—not always, and not on schedule:

- **The True Self becomes reachable.** You can ask it a question in the middle of chaos and actually hear what comes back. It's no longer a rare visitation—it's a line you know how to pick up, even if it doesn't always answer on the first ring.

- **Reactivity decreases dramatically.** You still have emotional responses, but there's space around them. You catch yourself before you say the thing you'll regret. Before you send the angry email. Before you collapse into the old pattern.

- **Presence comes easier.** You're in your head less. You're here more. You'll still lose it—but you'll notice sooner.

- **Equanimity deepens.** The highs don't send you to the moon. The lows don't destroy you. You're learning to surf the wavelength with grace.

- **Spontaneity returns.** Paradoxically, the more you listen, the more spontaneous you become. You're not planning every move. You're responding to what's alive in the moment. You're trusting the process.

This is the fruit of Teal. Not transcendence. Not escape. Just a deepening capacity to be fully human—awake, responsive, and free.
