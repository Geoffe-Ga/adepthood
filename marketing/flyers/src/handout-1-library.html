A journal that,
in time, answers back.

Adepthood is a private, encrypted journal. Write whatever you want, for as long as you want. As the entries accumulate, the app begins drawing on what you already wrote — returning your own past thinking to you as notes in the margin, at the moment it is relevant again. Not advice from a chatbot. Your words, handed back.

Yours, provably

Entries are stored encrypted. Export the lot — JSON, plus your journal as plain Markdown — or delete the account outright, from Settings. No request form, no waiting.

No streaks to keep

Nothing is gated, nothing is mandatory. The journal alone is the whole product if you want it to be. Habits, practices, a course and a community wait behind doors you open only if you feel like it.

Built to be outgrown

The stated goal is that you eventually stop needing it. No lock-in, no guilt mechanics, nothing that punishes you for leaving and taking your writing with you.
