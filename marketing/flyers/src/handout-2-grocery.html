Rising. Peaking. Withdrawal. Diminishing. Bottoming out. Restoration. Six phases that turn up in a mood, a creative project, a relationship, a season. The Archetypal Wavelength names them — and names, for each one, the difference between riding a phase and being flattened by it.

A map, not a mood ring

The Wavelength is the spine of APTITUDE, a ten-stage course, and of Adepthood, the app that paces it. Each of the ten stages reads all six phases in its own vocabulary.

Knowing which phase

Write in the app’s journal and your entries are sorted by phase and by stage, so the pattern you are living becomes something you can actually look at rather than only endure.

Contraction is not failure

There is no permanent peak on offer here. The bottom of the wave has its own medicine — convalescence — and the course treats it as a phase to be met well, not a lapse to be fixed.

Phase pairs use the Purple stage’s vocabulary; each of the ten stages names all six in its own terms. Adepthood is a framework for self-knowledge — it complements professional mental-health care and does not replace it.
