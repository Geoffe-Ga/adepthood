Aptitude
The course behind Adepthood
A ten-stage, thirty-six-week rite of passage

One to three minutes a day.
That’s where it starts.

APTITUDE brings ten human capacities online, one at a time, over 36 weeks. Each stage adds one habit and one practice, and the practice steps up only every three weeks — so you are never asked for more than the next small increment.

Agency · Receptivity · Self-Love · Community Love · Intellectual Understanding · Embodied Understanding · Systems Wisdom · True Self Wisdom · Unity · Emptiness

Eight stages · three weeks each The last two · six weeks each

“Stages organize the course.
Capacities organize a life.”

Not a ladder

No stage is higher than another. Wholeness means having the whole range available and knowing which part the moment is asking for. Then it begins again.

No guru

The author’s own line. The course hands you blueprints; you pick the materials. Every stage lists alternatives of the same length to swap in.

Householder, not monastic

What a monastery does with architecture, you do with habits: one new habit per stage, scaffolded so the stack never outruns your energy.
